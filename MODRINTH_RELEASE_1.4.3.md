# 1.4.3-1.21.11-(NeoForge)

## Custom Recipe — Minecraft 1.21.11 (NeoForge)

This update fixes recipe-book detection and placement for saved custom items and counted ingredients.

## Fixed

- The recipe book now recognizes saved custom items with their exact properties, including durability, when they are present in the inventory.
- Selecting a recipe now fills the crafting grid when the required custom items and configured quantities are available.
- Custom-item recipes no longer appear unavailable while the matching items are present.

## Compatibility / servers

- Recipe availability continues to use the exact properties of each saved custom item.

## Before updating

- Existing recipes and configurations remain compatible.
